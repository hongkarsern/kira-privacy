Privacy Policy for Kira

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Kira ("the app", "we", "us") is a group expense-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, used to create and identify your account.
  • Expense data: expense amounts, descriptions, and related details you or your group members enter into the app.
  • Diagnostic data: basic crash reports and performance data collected automatically to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To let you and the people in your group track and split shared expenses.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes or technical issues.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How We Store Your Information

Account and expense data is stored securely using Supabase, our backend database provider. Diagnostic and crash data is collected via Expo/EAS's built-in crash reporting tools.

Data Sharing

Your name and expense entries are visible to other members of the same group/trip you're part of, since that's the purpose of the app. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data may be shared with our service providers (Supabase, Expo/EAS) solely to operate the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, please email us at hongkarsern88@hotmail.com and we will process your request.

Children's Privacy

Kira is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hongkarsern88@hotmail.com.

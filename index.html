Privacy Policy

kira is a personal and shared expense tracker. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and the control you have over it — in plain language, no fine-print games.

Questions? Email hongkarsern3@gmail.com.

The short version. We don't sell your data. We don't run ads or track you across other apps. Your Face ID never leaves your phone, and we never see your card details. Everything below is the detail behind those promises.

01Who we are

kira is provided by Hong Kar Sern, an individual developer based in Malaysia. For the purposes of Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and, where it applies, the EU/UK GDPR, we are the data controller for the personal data described here. By creating an account, you agree to this policy.

03Payments

Purchases of kira Pro are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and entitlements are managed by RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your credit card or payment details — the app store you purchased through handles those. We receive only your subscription status (active or expired) and a purchase identifier.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the app.

05Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal data — transactions, budgets, receipts, and friends — is permanently deleted. Data you contributed to shared groups may remain visible to other members where it's needed for their own records (for example, a settled balance).

Diagnostic and aggregated analytics data may be retained by our providers for a limited period under their own retention policies.

06Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data — you can edit most of it directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Export your data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing such as notifications or analytics.

To exercise any of these, use the in-app controls or email hongkarsern3@gmail.com. We'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

07Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Profile → Delete Account in the app. This permanently erases your expenses, budgets, receipts, and friends, and cannot be undone. You may also request deletion by emailing us.

08Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by our providers with access controls and row-level security, so each user can only reach their own data and the data explicitly shared with them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

09Children's privacy

kira is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10International transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including the United States. Where required, we rely on our providers' appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notified in the app.
